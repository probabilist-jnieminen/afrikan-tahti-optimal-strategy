# Afrikan tähti: rules as implemented

Based on the official rules with the 2005 sea-travel amendment (`docs/context/afrikan-tahti-rules.pdf`), plus the interpretations our family plays with. Where the official text is silent or ambiguous, this file is authoritative.

## Components

- 2–6 players.
- 30 disks, one per red circle:
  - 1 African Star
  - 2 rubies
  - 3 emeralds
  - 4 topazes
  - 3 robbers
  - 5 horseshoes
  - 12 blanks

  The box contains 33 disks; the 3 spare blanks are not used.
- Each player starts with £300. The bank never runs out.

## Board terms

- **Location**: a start city (Tangier, Cairo), a red circle, a land dot or a sea dot.
- **Land line**: white line with black dots. **Sea line**: light-blue line with dark-blue dots. **Air line**: red line between two red circles (or a red circle and a start city, if the board has such lines).
- **Step**: one move to an adjacent location along a land or sea line.
- **Harbour**: a red circle or start city with a sea line attached.
- **Voyage**: a sea journey from one red circle or start city to the next along a sea line.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the disks face down and place one on each red circle. Nobody knows their contents.
2. Determine the turn order at random.
3. In turn order, each player chooses Tangier or Cairo as their starting location.

## A turn

At the start of the turn, the player chooses one of the following.

**A. Pay to open.** If standing on a red circle with an unopened disk, pay £100 and open it. The turn ends.

**B. Roll to open.** If standing on a red circle with an unopened disk, roll the die. On 4–6 the disk is opened for free. On 1–3 nothing happens. The turn ends either way.

**C. Roll.** Roll the die and, after seeing the result *r*, choose one of:

1. **Move *r* steps** along land and sea lines (see Movement).
2. **Fly** instead, ignoring the roll (see Flying).
3. **Stay.** Do not move; the turn ends.

**D. Free sail.** Available to any player, with or without money. The player must be standing in a harbour or on a sea dot. Move exactly 2 steps along sea lines without rolling. If a red circle or start city is reached after 1 step, the player may stop there. The second step cannot be used on land.

## Movement

- A move consists of exactly *r* steps, but the player may stop early at any red circle or start city. A move can end on a plain land or sea dot only if the steps run out exactly there.
- Within one move, the player may reverse direction and revisit locations.
- Passing through a red circle without stopping is allowed, even if its disk is unopened.
- **Sea fares.** Starting a voyage costs £100, paid when the player enters the sea line from a harbour. This is true both at the start of a move and mid-move: one move may combine land and sea in either order. Each new voyage costs another £100. Continuing a paid voyage begun on an earlier turn (standing on a sea dot) costs nothing.
- A player with £0 cannot start a voyage while moving by roll. They use Free sail instead.
- Any number of players may stand on the same location.

## Flying

- Pay £300 and fly along one air line from the current red circle to the red circle at its other end. No die roll. At most one flight per turn.
- Flying is decided after the roll (option C.2) and is also possible while held at St Helena (see Special locations).

## Opening a disk on arrival

When a move or flight ends on a red circle with an unopened disk, the player may pay £100 to open it immediately. The turn then ends. If the player does not pay, the turn ends, and next turn they may use option A or B.

## Disk effects

| Disk | Effect |
|---|---|
| Blank | Removed. |
| Horseshoe | If nobody has found the Star yet: removed, no effect. If the Star has been found: the player now holds a winning horseshoe. |
| Robber | The player loses all their money. |
| Topaz | +£300 (doubled at Gold Coast). |
| Emerald | +£600 (doubled at Gold Coast). |
| Ruby | +£1000 (doubled at Gold Coast). |
| African Star | The player holds the Star. |

## Special locations

- **Cape Town.** The first player to reach Cape Town gets £500. Passing through counts.
- **Gold Coast.** Gems opened here pay double.
- **Slave Coast.** A player who opens a blank here loses their next 3 turns, whether the disk was paid for or won by rolling. Other disks work normally.
- **St Helena.** On arriving at the island by any means, the player is held by pirates. At the start of each turn while held, the player chooses exactly one of:
  - **Roll to escape.** On 1 or 2 the player is released and immediately sails that many steps. The player chooses either to pay the £100 fare, making it a normal paid voyage, or not to pay, making it a free voyage that continues with Free sail (2 steps per turn) on later turns. On 3–6 the turn ends.
  - **Fly** away (£300) without rolling.
  - **Option A or B** if the island's disk is unopened.

  One roll cannot serve two purposes.
- **Sahara.** A player whose move *ends* on the marked Sahara dot is held by bedouins. Passing over the dot has no effect. At the start of each turn, the held player rolls. On a 1 or 2 they are released and immediately move that many steps. On 3–6 the turn ends. The dot is not a red circle, so flying is not possible there.

## Winning

- The first player to reach Tangier or Cairo wins, provided they hold the Star or a winning horseshoe.
- An exact roll is not needed: entering the city ends the move and wins the game.
